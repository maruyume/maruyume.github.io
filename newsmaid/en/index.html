Today’s topics, served by your maid.

News Maid is an iPhone app for reading original articles created by its operator. It delivers approachable explanations of timely topics as a maid’s letter to her master.

Read now, save for later

  • Original articles are delivered from the operator-managed server with a title, introduction, body, and reference information.

  • You can save articles and manage their read status locally on your device.

  • Reference links in an article let you check related external information.

Advertising

Advertising delivery is not currently enabled in the public app. Google test ads may appear in TestFlight builds during verification. This page and the Privacy Policy will be updated before live advertising is enabled.

Before you use the app

Articles are provided for informational purposes. For important decisions, please consult reference material, official announcements, and qualified professionals. An internet connection is required to retrieve articles.
