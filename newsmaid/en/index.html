News, neatly served.

News Maid is an iPhone news app for discovering topics through headlines and publisher-provided excerpts, then opening the original articles. This website provides support information, our Privacy Policy, and Terms of Use.

Find what matters, in less time

  • Browse categories and search for articles that interest you.

  • Organize articles with bookmarks and read status.

  • Read information from sources whose reuse terms have been checked, with attribution and links to the originals. Coverage does not include every publisher or region.

  • For eligible articles, explore key points and related topics using the provided headlines and excerpts. We do not retrieve or store article bodies without permission.

AI features

Supported Apple Intelligence devices can use on-device processing through Foundation Models. Where unavailable because of device, OS, language, or settings, free rule-based processing is used instead. No external paid AI API or Private Cloud Compute is used. Generated content may contain errors; always check the original article for important information.

Before you use the app

The app is ad-supported and its core features are free. An internet connection is required to fetch news. Article language is separate from the app's interface language; the app does not automatically translate every article.
