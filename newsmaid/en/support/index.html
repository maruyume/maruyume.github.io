News Maid Support

For questions, feedback, bug reports, or rights-related inquiries, please use the contact form below. This is the same contact channel listed in the app's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Contact form (external website)

Please mention “News Maid” and include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce the issue, and any error message. Do not send unnecessary personal information, Apple Account passwords, or device identifiers. The external website and form provider's privacy policies apply when you use their services.

No news appears

Check your internet connection, selected category and news language, and whether the RSS source is enabled. Try refreshing. Sources may stop working or change their feed format. Coverage does not include every region or category.

AI is unavailable

Foundation Models requires a supported device and OS, enabled Apple Intelligence, supported language, and other availability conditions. Rule-based processing is used where AI is unavailable. Unverified feeds, including user-added RSS, are excluded from AI processing.

Deleting data and iCloud sync

The Settings action to delete all local data clears saved data and generated-result caches on your device. Do not treat this as bulk deletion of your iCloud data. Turning off sync alone does not immediately delete data already stored in iCloud. For iCloud deletion, consult the management methods provided by Apple. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Notifications do not arrive

Check notification permission for News Maid in your iPhone settings and notification settings inside the app. These are local reminders, not a guaranteed nationwide breaking-news or emergency-alert delivery service.

Advertising and privacy choices

Check the app's Settings for available advertising privacy choices. Tracking permission can be changed in your iPhone settings. Basic features such as reading news remain available without tracking permission.

Publishers and rights holders

For questions about displayed content or attribution, please include the original article URL, publisher name, and details of your inquiry. Uses allowed by licenses or the law are not restricted.

Privacy Policy / Terms of Use

This support website does not embed advertisements, analytics, or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. External websites have their own privacy policies.
