News Maid Support

For questions, feedback, bug reports, or rights-related inquiries, please use the contact form below. This is the same contact channel listed in the app's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Contact form (external website)

Please mention “News Maid” and include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce the issue, and any error message. Do not send unnecessary personal information, Apple Account passwords, or device identifiers. The external website and form provider's privacy policies apply when you use their services.

Articles do not appear

Check your internet connection, then try “Refresh articles” in the app’s Settings. The latest articles may be temporarily unavailable during server maintenance or a network interruption.

Deleting saved data

You can separately delete saved articles and read history from the app’s Settings. These records are stored locally on your device.

Advertising and privacy choices

When advertising is enabled, available privacy choices can be reviewed from the app’s Settings in applicable regions. Core features such as reading articles remain available without tracking permission.

Publishers and rights holders

For questions about an article or its reference information, please include the applicable article title or URL and the details you would like us to review.

Privacy Policy / Terms of Use

This support website does not embed advertisements, analytics, or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. External websites have their own privacy policies.
