Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store bookmarks, read history, and display settings locally on your device to provide its features. You can delete them from Settings.

2. Retrieving Articles

The App retrieves article data from a server managed by the operator. During network communication, IP addresses and other communication-related information may be handled by telecommunications providers or the server provider.

3. Advertising

When advertising is enabled, the App uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform. In applicable regions, it presents advertising consent and privacy choices. Google test ads are used while verifying TestFlight builds.

4. Tracking Permission

When advertising requires tracking, the App presents Apple’s permission prompt. Core article-reading features remain available if permission is not granted.

5. External Links

The privacy policies of external sites apply when you open a reference link in an article.

6. Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated as necessary. Please contact us at nokt1220.com/contact/.

This support website does not embed advertisements, analytics, or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. External websites have their own privacy policies.
