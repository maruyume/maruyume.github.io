Privacy Policy

Establishment date: June 16, 2026 Last updated: September 17, 2026

The operator of News Maid (hereinafter referred to as “we”) has established the following privacy policy (hereinafter referred to as “this policy”) regarding the handling of user information in the news viewing application “News Maid” (hereinafter referred to as “this application”) provided by us.

Article 1 Information to be acquired

This application may obtain or store the following information.

1. Information saved by users within this app

This app may store the following information on your device to improve user convenience.

  1. Information about bookmarked articles

  2. Read history

  3. Display categories

  4. Sort order of categories

  5. Settings regarding topics you are interested in and topics you are not interested in

  6. In-app display settings

  7. Last updated date and time

  8. Other setting information necessary to provide the functions of this application

As a general rule, this information is stored on the user's device. If the user enables iCloud sync in Settings, the app stores bookmarked article identifiers, headlines, sources, links and publication dates, required attribution such as author names and licences, read article identifiers, display categories, interest categories, and added RSS settings in the user's private iCloud database. Article bodies, article images, and inputs used for AI processing are not included in iCloud sync.

2. Information generated when acquiring news

This app may obtain news information from RSS, public feeds, external news sites, etc.

In this case, due to the communication mechanism, the user's IP address, device information, access date and time, etc. may be processed by us, external news sources, external sites, telecommunications carriers, etc.

3. Information when opening an external article

When a user opens an external news site, advertisement, external link, etc. from this application, cookies, device information, access information, browsing history, advertising identifiers, etc. may be obtained from the external site.

Regarding the handling of information on external sites, please check the privacy policy, terms of use, cookie policy, etc. of each external site.

4. Information obtained when making inquiries

When users contact us, we may collect the following information:

  1. Email address

  2. Inquiry details

  3. Information required for reply

  4. Device information, OS version, app version, etc. required for bug investigation

5. Information regarding advertisement distribution

This application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

The advertising provider may obtain the following information to display advertisements, measure advertising effectiveness, prevent unauthorized use, and improve advertising quality.

  1. IP address

  2. Device information

  3. Advertising identifier

  4. App usage status

  5. Information regarding advertisement display/clicks, etc.

  6. Crash log

  7. Performance data

  8. Approximate location information

  9. Other information specified by the advertising distribution company

If tracking using advertising identifiers is required, this app may request permission from the user based on iOS App Tracking Transparency. Even if the user does not give permission, the basic functions of this app can be used, but the content of advertisements displayed may not be personalized.

In regions where consent or privacy choices are legally required, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and applicable U.S. states, we use Google User Messaging Platform to display an advertising consent message or privacy choices. Where required, users can review or change those choices from Settings.

6. On-device summaries and AI processing

The app generates summaries and key points from headlines and RSS excerpts whose usage terms permit processing. When Apple Intelligence is available on a supported device and OS, it uses Apple's Foundation Models for on-device processing. Otherwise, it uses local rule-based processing. This feature does not send inputs to an operator-operated server or a paid external AI API. Private Cloud Compute is currently disabled. User-added and unverified feeds are excluded from AI processing.

Up to 100 generated results are cached locally. Results generated within the last seven days may be reused; reaching seven days does not automatically delete stored data. The Settings action to delete all local data also clears this cache. Generated results may contain errors; please check the original article.

Article 2 Purpose of use

We will use the information we obtain or store for the following purposes:

  1. To provide, maintain, protect, and improve this application

  2. To display news lists, article details, summaries, related topics, etc.

  3. To save and display bookmarks, read history, category settings, etc.

  4. To investigate app defects, improve quality, and confirm usage status.

  5. To respond to inquiries from users

  6. To display advertisements, measure advertising effectiveness, prevent unauthorized use, and improve advertising quality.

  7. To respond to violations of terms of use, unauthorized use, and security issues

  8. To respond to laws and requests from public institutions

Article 3 Use of third party services

This app uses the following third-party services.

Google AdMob

This app uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform for advertisement distribution and privacy choices.

Google or a third party involved in ad distribution may obtain device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad display/click information, application usage status, etc. for purposes such as ad distribution, measurement of ad effectiveness, prevention of unauthorized use, and improvement of ad quality.

For information on how Google handles information, please refer to Google's privacy policy and explanation about Google AdMob.

External news site/RSS source

This application may obtain and display information from RSS, public feeds, external news sites, etc.

When a user opens an external article, the handling of information on that external site will follow the privacy policy, etc. of each external site.

Apple iCloud and CloudKit

If the user enables iCloud sync, this app uses Apple's CloudKit to store the synchronization data described in Article 1 in the user's private iCloud database. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to information handled by CloudKit.

Article 4 Third party provision

We will not provide users' personal information to third parties except in the following cases.

  1. With user consent

  2. When based on laws and regulations

  3. When necessary for the protection of human life, body or property

  4. When it is particularly necessary to improve public health or promote the healthy development of children.

  5. When cooperation with national agencies, local governments, or those entrusted by them is required.

  6. When having a subcontractor or third-party service handle information to the extent necessary for the operation of this application.

Article 5 Information retention period

Bookmarks, read history, category settings, etc. saved on the device may be saved until the user deletes them within this app or uninstalls this app. If iCloud sync is enabled, synced data may remain until the user deletes the relevant data in this app or deletes this app's iCloud data using a method provided by Apple. Turning off sync alone does not immediately delete data already stored in iCloud.

Even after a response has been completed, information related to an inquiry may be retained for a necessary period of time for confirmation, re-responding, trouble prevention, etc.

The storage period of information acquired by advertising distribution services, external news sites, and third-party services is subject to the policies of each service.

Article 6 User management

Users can perform the following operations using the settings screen within this app or the device settings.

  1. Delete bookmarks

  2. Delete read history

  3. Change display category

  4. Change notification settings

  5. Change Ad Tracking Permissions

  6. Deleting data on the device by uninstalling the app

  7. Enable or disable iCloud sync

You can change iOS ad tracking permissions from your device's settings app.

Article 7 About notifications

This app provides local notifications such as saved article reminders.

When using notifications, this application may ask the user for permission to receive notifications. Users can allow, deny, or change notifications by setting their device.

Article 8 Use by minors

If a minor user uses this app, they must obtain the consent of their parent or other legal representative.

We do not provide this app for the purpose of knowingly acquiring personal information from minors.

Article 9 Safety management

We will endeavor to take reasonable security control measures to prevent the information we have obtained from leakage, loss, falsification, unauthorized access, etc.

However, due to the nature of internet communications, external services, terminal environments, etc., we cannot completely guarantee the security of information.

Article 10 Changes to the privacy policy

We may change this policy as necessary.

If we change this policy, we will display the revised content and effective date within this application or by any other method we deem appropriate.

To the extent possible, we will notify users of any material changes in a way that is easy to understand.
