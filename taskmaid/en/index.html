A gentle way to get things done

Plan your day with your maid.

TaskMaid is an iPhone to-do list where a charming maid helps you sort out today. Widgets, reminders and a focus timer keep every day moving, one task at a time.

Take a look

What you can do

  • Today at a glanceSee today's tasks and progress on the Home screen and in widgets, and check tasks off right from the widget.
  • Reminders and repeatsReminders with or without a due date. Repeat daily, on weekdays, weekly, monthly or yearly.
  • Focus timer25, 45 or 60 minutes. It keeps running when your iPhone is locked and lets you know when time's up.
  • Chat with your maidGet help planning your day and receive task suggestions (iOS 27 or later, on iPhone models with Apple Intelligence).
  • Siri and ShortcutsJust say "Add a task in TaskMaid".
  • BackupExport your tasks to a file and restore them on a new iPhone. No account needed.
