PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 9, 2026 / Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. Introduction

TaskMaid (the “App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information stored on your device

Task details you enter—including titles, notes, due dates, priorities, categories and completion status—as well as App preferences and achievement data are generally stored only on your device. The App does not require an account, does not provide cloud synchronization, and does not send this content to the operator’s server.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App creates local notifications on your device based on task due dates and related settings. You can change notification permission in the App or in iPhone Settings.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform SDK. For ad delivery, consent management, measurement, fraud prevention and service improvement, these services may collect or process information such as IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device information and identifiers, advertising data, interactions with ads and the App, crash logs, diagnostics and performance data.

Where required by regional law, the App uses Google User Messaging Platform to present choices regarding the storage of or access to information on a device and the processing of personal data for advertising.

Before a device identifier may be used for personalized advertising or advertising measurement, the App requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can use the IDFA only when the required regional consent has been provided and App Tracking Transparency permission has been granted. If tracking is not allowed, the App does not use the IDFA and disables ad personalization. The App's core features remain fully available.

The Google Mobile Ads publisher first-party identifier remains disabled regardless of the user's choice. Non-personalized ads may still process information needed for ad delivery, frequency capping, aggregate reporting and fraud prevention.

For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Support inquiries

If you contact us, your email address, message and any attachment you choose to provide are transmitted through your selected email service. We use this information only to respond and provide support.

6. Retention and deletion

On-device task information remains until you delete it or remove the App. Information received through a support inquiry is kept only as long as necessary to respond and meet legal obligations, then deleted appropriately.

7. Your choices

You can disable notifications in the App or iPhone Settings, and delete tasks using the App’s data management controls. Where required, the App provides an advertising privacy options entry point in Settings. You can change App Tracking Transparency permission in iPhone Settings.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss of information under our control.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the App, third-party services or applicable laws change. Material changes will be announced in the App or through another appropriate method.
