PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 9, 2026 / Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

TaskMaid (the “App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information stored on your device

Task details you enter—including titles, notes, due dates, priorities, categories and completion status—as well as App preferences and achievement data are generally stored only on your device. The App does not require an account and does not send this content to the operator’s server. If iCloud Sync is on, it is also stored in your own iCloud (see below). For how information is handled when you use the maid chat, see section 5.

To show them in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, the titles, due dates and completion status of tasks around today are also stored in an area on your device that only the App and its widgets can read. Adding a task with Siri or Shortcuts is also processed on your device.

If you choose Export Backup, a file containing your tasks and settings is saved to the location you pick, such as the Files app or iCloud Drive. This file is never sent to the operator. Files saved to iCloud Drive are handled under the terms and privacy policy of Apple’s service.

If you use Import from Reminders, the App reads your unfinished reminders (title, notes, due date, priority and list name) with your permission and saves them as tasks in the App. This happens on your device only and nothing is sent to the operator.

If you turn on Show iPhone Calendar Events in Settings, the App reads the events in your iPhone’s Calendar (title, date and time, and calendar color) with your permission and shows them in the Calendar tab. They are read on your device only; events are not saved, changed or sent to the operator. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

iCloud Sync: When iCloud Sync is on (it is on by default), your tasks and some preferences (categories, badges and records, templates, and maid affinity) are stored in the private iCloud area of your Apple Account and synced between your devices that use the same Apple Account. This data is stored in Apple’s iCloud; the operator cannot see or access it. Its handling in iCloud follows Apple’s privacy policy. You can turn sync off in the App’s Settings at any time.

Photos and places: When you attach a photo to a task, the photo you choose is saved with the task on your device (and in your own iCloud when iCloud Sync is on). Only the photos you pick in the iPhone photo picker are read; no other photos are accessed. When you use Notify at a Place, the name and coordinates of the place you choose are saved with the task, and your iPhone determines on the device whether you have arrived there and notifies you. Using your current location requires your permission, and location information is never sent to the operator. Words you type to search for a place are sent to Apple’s map search to show results. When you choose “Use Current Location”, that location is sent to Apple Maps to look up the place’s name (such as its address).

To make them available in the iPhone’s search (Spotlight) and Siri, the titles of open tasks are also added to a search index on your device. This index never leaves your device.

Ad removal and tips (Treat Your Maid to Tea) are purchased through Apple’s App Store. The operator never receives your payment information.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App creates local notifications on your device based on task due dates, arrival at places you choose and related settings. You can change notification permission in the App or in iPhone Settings.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform SDK. For ad delivery, consent management, measurement, fraud prevention and service improvement, these services may collect or process information such as IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device information and identifiers, advertising data, interactions with ads and the App, crash logs, diagnostics and performance data.

Where required by regional law, the App uses Google User Messaging Platform to present choices regarding the storage of or access to information on a device and the processing of personal data for advertising.

Before a device identifier may be used for personalized advertising or advertising measurement, the App requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can use the IDFA only when the required regional consent has been provided and App Tracking Transparency permission has been granted. If tracking is not allowed, the App does not use the IDFA and disables ad personalization. The App's core features remain fully available.

The Google Mobile Ads publisher first-party identifier remains disabled regardless of the user's choice. Non-personalized ads may still process information needed for ad delivery, frequency capping, aggregate reporting and fraud prevention.

For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Maid chat (AI feature)

The App includes a chat feature that lets you ask your maid questions and talk things through. When you use it, the following information is sent to Apple’s Private Cloud Compute and processed to create a reply:

  • The messages you enter and the earlier messages in the same conversation
  • Your unfinished tasks (up to 30, soonest due first): title, due date, priority, category, repeat setting, whether the task is overdue, and up to the first 80 characters of its notes
  • The current date and time, the name of your selected maid, and the App’s display language

Private Cloud Compute is Apple’s server system designed with privacy in mind. The information sent is used only to create the reply, is not stored after processing, and cannot be viewed by anyone, including Apple. This information is never sent to the operator’s server. Conversations are not saved on your device either and disappear when you close the chat screen.

The feature appears only on devices that support Apple Intelligence and have Apple Intelligence turned on. If you don’t use it, none of the information above is sent. For how Apple handles information, see Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Support inquiries

If you contact us, your email address, message and any attachment you choose to provide are transmitted through your selected email service. We use this information only to respond and provide support.

7. Retention and deletion

On-device task information remains until you delete it or remove the App. Tasks stored in iCloud remain in your iCloud until you delete them. Information received through a support inquiry is kept only as long as necessary to respond and meet legal obligations, then deleted appropriately. Maid chat conversations are not stored.

8. Your choices

You can disable notifications in the App or iPhone Settings, and delete tasks using the App’s data management controls. The maid chat is optional; if you don’t use it, no task information is sent. Where required, the App provides an advertising privacy options entry point in Settings. You can change App Tracking Transparency and location permissions in iPhone Settings.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss of information under our control.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the App, third-party services or applicable laws change. Material changes will be announced in the App or through another appropriate method.
